Panel guide

The comic's small lettering can be hard to read on a phone. This guide follows the strip panel by panel.

  1. A young person faces a judge’s gavel and a watching crowd. The words begin, “Because where I come from...”
  2. Two hands hold images of jail and death as the choices handed to “us.”
  3. A white boy looks ahead in a bright outdoor scene.
  4. The same boy looks toward an American flag. The caption says the American Dream was the choice handed to “them.”
  5. The first young person speaks back: “So here we are—”
  6. Lady Justice wears a blindfold and raises a gavel. In the final red image, the young person says, “I see you.”

Made by Daivik Lakkisetty Naga and Micha Ross. Inspired by Punching the Air.
