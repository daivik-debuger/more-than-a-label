The comic

Because where I come from...

A six-panel visual response to “Blind Justice” from Punching the Air.

Read alongside the art

Panel guide

The lettering in the original drawing is small. This guide describes the visual story so it is easier to follow on a phone or with a screen reader.

  1. A young person is shown at the start of a road. The scene asks us to think about where he comes from.
  2. Two harsh choices are pictured: jail and death. The comic shows how limited his future can feel.
  3. The character looks up, still carrying those choices with him.
  4. An American flag appears. It suggests the promise of opportunity that other people may receive.
  5. He speaks up: this is where his story has brought him.
  6. Lady Justice wears a blindfold. The ending asks whether justice really sees everyone the same way.

What the comic asks

Who gets room to dream, and who is expected to defend their humanity first?
